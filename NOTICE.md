# Data sources and licences

The MIT licence in LICENSE applies to the original project code, not to the third-party linguistic data.

The Japanese/English vocabulary is derived from [JMdict / EDRDG](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), copyright James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, under the [EDRDG licence / CC BY-SA 4.0](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/newlic.html). The selection and data structure were changed. Public vocabulary was updated against the 2026-10-26 English JSON release of [jmdict-simplified](https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified).

The Japanese and English example sentences are derived from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/), generally under [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Sentence-level contributor names and links to the original Japanese and English sentences are included in the published data and displayed in the application. The selection and data structure were changed. The source files and matching procedure are described in `datasets/published/README.md`.
